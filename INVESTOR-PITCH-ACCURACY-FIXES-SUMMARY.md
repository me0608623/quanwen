## Four Critical Accuracy Issues — All Fixed

### Issue 1: ARR Inconsistency (30-67x gap) — FIXED ✅

**Problem:** Elevator pitch stated NT$100-200M Year 1 ARR, but execution timeline showed Q4 NT$3M ARR.

**Root Cause:** Confusion between optimistic projections and actual achievable milestones.

**Fixed in v1.1:**

| Section | Before | After |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Elevator Pitch | NT$100-200M ARR | NT$3-5M ARR（Q4 末里程碑） |
| Slide 3 SOM Year 1 | NT$100-200M | NT$3-5M ARR（與 Q4 里程碑一致：20-30 簽約） |
| Slide 11 Runway | NT$100-200M ARR | NT$3-5M ARR（Q4 末）；累計實收 NT$1-2M |
| FAQ Q5 | Defended high number | Clarified build-up: Q4 NT$3M → Year 2 NT$30-50M → Year 3 NT$500M+ |

**Impact:** Medium — Foundational credibility issue. Investors spot inconsistencies immediately.

**Problem:** "某大型 FMCG 企業…損失 NT$50M" cited with no source, no client name, no date — fabrication risk.

**Root Cause:** Illustrative example presented without clear markers.

**Fixed in v1.1:**

| Section | Before | After |
|---------|--------|-------|

**Impact:** Critical — Unattributed examples are red flags. Must be replaced with real verified case studies before any investor pitch.

### Issue 4: Year 2-3 Revenue Inconsistency (16-27x gap) — FIXED ✅ (v1.2)

**Problem:** Slide 12 showed Year 2 Revenue NT$800M-1B, but Key Assumptions said Year 2 ARR NT$30-50M — a 16-27x gap.

**Root Cause:** Copy-paste error or misaligned forecasting between team members. The FAQ (Q5) had the correct logic.

**Fixed in v1.2:**

| Section | Before | After |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Slide 12 Year 2 | Revenue：NT$800M-1B | Revenue：NT$30-50M ARR（符合關鍵假設：100+ 簽約） |
| Slide 12 Year 3 | Revenue：NT$3-5B | Revenue：NT$500M-1B（區域擴展，含亞洲市場） |
