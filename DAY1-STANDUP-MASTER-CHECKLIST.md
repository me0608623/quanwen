## Standup Agenda (9:30-10:00am)

### Opening (1 min)
```
"Welcome to Day 1 execution. All systems appear operational from my check.
Let's confirm status from each team and identify any blockers.
Sales Lead, you first."
```

### Sales Lead Report (5 min)
**Questions to ask:**
1. CRM system online and working? Email tracking active?
2. Cold email lists verified and ready to send?
3. Any technical issues or concerns?
4. Ready to send first 80 cold emails today?

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ CRM online and tested
- ✅ Email lists confirmed
- ✅ Team trained and ready
- ✅ Day 1 target: 80 cold emails

**If Issues:**
- 🟡 Minor issue (e.g., template needs small fix): Fix by 11am, continue on schedule
- 🔴 Critical issue (e.g., CRM down, email lists missing): Escalate to CEO immediately, activate fallback

### Growth Lead Report (5 min)
**Questions to ask:**
1. Respondent onboarding working end-to-end?
2. Dcard post scheduled and live?
3. Email automation sequences active?
4. Any technical issues?

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ Signup → Profile → Survey → Reward flow working
- ✅ Social posts queued or ready to publish
- ✅ Email automation live
- ✅ Day 1 target: 200+ new respondents

**If Issues:**
- 🟡 Minor issue (e.g., onboarding button needs tweaking): Fix by noon, continue on schedule
- 🔴 Critical issue (e.g., signup page broken, emails not sending): Escalate to CEO immediately, activate fallback

### Content Lead Report (5 min)
**Questions to ask:**
1. Blog #1 published or ready for Day 1 morning?
2. Blog platform loading fast?
3. Email signup capture working?
4. LinkedIn ads campaign live and getting impressions?

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ Blog #1 live (or confirmed for Day 1 morning)
- ✅ Platform performance: <2s load time
- ✅ Email capture functional
- ✅ LinkedIn ads active
- ✅ Day 1 target: 500+ views, 50+ email signups

**If Issues:**
- 🟡 Minor issue (e.g., blog post not yet published): Publish by noon, continue on schedule
- 🔴 Critical issue (e.g., blog platform down, ads not running): Escalate to CEO immediately, activate fallback

### VP Marketing Summary (5 min)
**Status Summary:**
```
"Day 1 status: [GREEN/YELLOW/RED]
- All systems operational: [YES/NO]
- Any critical blockers: [NONE/LIST ITEMS]
- Team readiness: [READY/CONCERNS]
- Go/No-Go Decision: [GO/CONDITIONAL/NO-GO]

```

**Decision Framework:**
- **🟢 GREEN:** All systems operational, all teams ready → GO (begin Day 1 execution at full pace)
- **🟡 YELLOW:** Minor issues identified, but fixable within 2 hours → CONDITIONAL GO (continue with caution, resolve issues by 11am)
- **🔴 RED:** Critical blocker exists (CRM down, onboarding broken) → NO-GO (escalate to CEO, discuss delay or fallback activation)

**Day 1 Targets (If GO):**
- Sales: 80 cold emails sent
- Growth: 200+ respondents acquired
- Content: Blog #1 live, 500+ views

## Post-Standup (10:00-10:15am) — Action Items

### If 🟢 GO

1. **Immediate (10:00am):**
   - [ ] Sales Lead: Begin cold email send (target: 50 by noon, 80 by EOD)
   - [ ] Growth Lead: Publish Dcard post (if not auto-published)
   - [ ] Content Lead: Publish Blog #1 (if not live)

2. **First Check-In (12:00pm):**
   - Sales Lead: Report cold emails sent (should be ~50)
   - Growth Lead: Report respondents acquired (should be ~100)
   - Content Lead: Report blog views (should be ~200)

3. **EOD Report (5:00pm):**
   - Full Day 1 KPI summary per WEEK1-DAILY-EXECUTION-LOG.md
   - Variance analysis & recommendations for Day 2

### If 🟡 CONDITIONAL GO

1. **Immediate (10:00am):**
   - [ ] Identify issue owner
   - [ ] Set 2-hour fix deadline (11am latest)
   - [ ] Activate parallel workaround if needed
   - [ ] Notify CEO of issue + ETA for fix

2. **Status Check (11:00am):**
   - Issue resolved? → Resume full execution
   - Still in progress? → Escalate to CEO, activate fallback plan

### If 🔴 NO-GO

1. **Immediate (10:00am):**
   - [ ] VP Marketing notifies CEO
   - [ ] Discuss delay option (24-48 hours) vs fallback activation
   - [ ] Document blocker in WEEK1-LAUNCH-BLOCKER-ASSESSMENT.md
   - [ ] CEO makes go/no-go decision

2. **Decision Outcome:**
   - **Option A (Delay):** Reschedule Day 1 for 2026-06-02 or 2026-06-03
   - **Option B (Fallback):** Activate contingency plan per WEEK1-LAUNCH-BLOCKER-ASSESSMENT.md
