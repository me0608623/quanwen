## Daily Standup — 9:30am (All Team Leads + VP Marketing)

**Agenda Format (30 minutes):**

1. **Sales Lead** (5 min): Cold email sent? Discovery calls booked? Any blockers?
2. **Growth Lead** (5 min): Respondents acquired? Social engagement? Funnel health?
3. **Content Lead** (5 min): Blog published? Traffic? Email captures?
4. **VP Marketing** (10 min): Overall variance analysis, red/yellow/black alerts, Day 2 adjustments
5. **Open Issues** (5 min): Blockers, escalations, CEO decision items

**Decision Framework:**

- **Green (On Track):** 90-110% of daily target → No action needed
- **Yellow (Caution):** 70-89% of target → Alert and prepare adjustment
- **Red (Critical):** <70% of target → Escalate immediately, activate backup plan

**Example Red Flag Responses:**

- Sales <50 emails sent → Review CRM, activate backup email system, increase team send rate
- Growth <200 respondents → Increase LinkedIn ads by 50%, review onboarding, accelerate social posts
- Content blog not published → All hands on deck to publish by noon, extend timeline communication

## Contingency Activation (If Blockers Detected)

### 🔴 If Sales Outreach Fails

- **Blocker:** CRM down, email system broken
- **Fallback 1:** Manual cold email from personal accounts (Gmail)
- **Fallback 2:** Direct LinkedIn outreach (slower, less scalable)
- **Fallback 3:** CEO makes warm intros to 20+ investors/advisors for list
- **Decision:** Sales Lead + CEO call by 11am, execute within 1 hour

### 🔴 If Growth Onboarding Broken

- **Blocker:** Signup page errors, email not sending
- **Fallback 1:** Manual respondent registration via admin panel (slower)
- **Fallback 2:** Google Forms + manual wallet credit (less smooth)
- **Fallback 3:** Pause new signups, fix onboarding, restart Day 2
- **Decision:** Engineering + Growth Lead call by 11am, target 2-hour fix

### 🔴 If Content Not Published

- **Blocker:** Blog platform issue, post not ready
- **Fallback 1:** Publish on Medium or LinkedIn directly (same content)
- **Fallback 2:** Email blog post to entire lead list manually
- **Fallback 3:** Create TL;DR infographic on Dcard/PTT instead
- **Decision:** Content Lead emergency action, publish by noon latest
