## Phase 2: Collect Real Case Stories (Week 2 — By 2026-06-14)

**Goal:** Conduct interviews, record testimonials, draft case studies

### Case Story Format

**Each case study should include:**

1. **Customer Profile** (2-3 sentences)
   - Company name, industry, team size
   - Use case (market research, product feedback, customer surveys)
   - Example: "Fortune 500 FMCG company, 50-person research team, conducting 20+ annual product surveys"

2. **Challenge** (2-3 sentences)
   - What was the problem before QuanWen?
   - How much time/cost/quality impact?
   - Example: "Spent 3 weeks manually reviewing responses for each survey, losing 20% of responses to spam/low-quality entries"

3. **Solution** (2-3 sentences)
   - How did QuanWen help?
   - What was the implementation?
   - Example: "Deployed QuanWen AI audit on 5,000 responses, automatically filtered 850 spam entries (17%), reduced review time to 2 days"

4. **Results** (Quantified)
   - Time saved: X hours → X days
   - Cost saved: X% reduction
   - Quality improvement: X% increase in data quality
   - Example: "85% reduction in review time (21 days → 3 days), 20% improvement in data quality"

5. **Quote** (1 sentence from customer)
   - Customer name, title
   - Example: "QuanWen's AI caught spam patterns we would have missed manually. We're using it for all future surveys." — Jane Doe, Research Manager

## Contingency: If Real Cases Not Available

**Worst case:** No Beta customers willing to share case stories

### Fallback Options

**Option A: Remove FMCG story entirely** (By 6/7)
- Delete Slide 2 problem statement with case story
- Replace with generic problem statement (no specific company)
- Mention: "Available case studies upon request" in appendix
- Impact: Weaker emotional hook, but no credibility risk
- Timeline: 1 day to update

**Option B: Use anonymized customer quotes** (By 6/10)
- Conduct customer interviews (same process)
- Publish results without company name
- Example: "A Fortune 500 FMCG company saw 85% time savings..."
- Impact: Credible and specific, less impressive branding
- Timeline: 1 week to collect + anonymize

**Option C: Early access partner case study** (By 6/14)
- Propose exclusive partnership to 1 Beta customer
- Offer: 2 months free + co-marketing rights
- Get signed case study + testimonial
- Impact: Strong and credible, but requires partner negotiation
- Timeline: 2 weeks if partner agrees quickly

**Decision Logic:**
- Prefer Option A (real cases) if 3+ customers agree
- Fall back to Option B (anonymized) if 1-2 customers agree
- Pursue Option C (partnership) only if Option A/B won't work

**CEO Decision:** By 6/10, confirm which fallback to pursue
